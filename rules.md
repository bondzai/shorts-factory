# Rules

Read by the Idea agent and the Metadata agent on every run. Rewritten by the
Analyst agent — but only once enough clips have been published to justify a
change (`[analyst] min_published_for_rules` in `config.toml`). Until then
everything here is an untested hypothesis, and that is fine.

## Hook (first 1.5 seconds)

- Motion must already be happening on frame 1. No static intro, no title card.
- Whatever the viewer is asked to predict must be visible immediately.

## Title (English)

- 40-70 characters, sentence case, no stacked punctuation.
- Ask a question the clip answers, or name the outcome without spoiling it.
- No Thai characters in the title, description, or hashtags.

## Hashtags

- 3 to 5 tags: one format tag (#shorts), one niche tag, the rest specific.

## Variety

- Do not repeat the same variant more than twice in any 7 consecutive clips.
