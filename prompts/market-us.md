# Market: United States

The channel is built for US viewers on the Shorts feed, most of them scrolling
in the evening (US evening is 06:00–10:00 the next morning in Bangkok — that
is the publish window). Everything an agent writes is judged by that reader.

- **American English, always.** "color", "favorite"; no British spellings, no
  Thai, no transliterated words.
- **Frame it like sport.** US viewers read a race as a bracket: "heat", "final",
  "upset", "by a nose", "photo finish". Use those words; they are free stakes.
- **Verbs over adjectives.** "Pick your marble" beats "crazy stage"; never
  "nail-biting", "insane", "epic". The margin is never in the title, and a
  parts list ("3 spinners, 24 bumpers") is not a hook.
- **Titles**: sentence case, the pick in the first three words ("Pick",
  "Call it", "Which one is yours"), the lineup named, never the winner or the
  margin, under 60 characters so nothing is cut on a phone. A question only when the
  clip answers it in the next fifteen seconds.
- **Pinned comment** is a pick-your-team prompt with the colours named:
  "Red, blue or green — which did you back?" That is the whole engagement play.
- **Hashtags**: `#shorts` plus two the US audience actually follows —
  `#satisfying`, `#marblerun`, `#marblerace`; never a hashtag with a brand or a
  person in it.
- **Never** reference Thai holidays, Thai prices, or "our country". A US-themed
  window (Halloween, Christmas, New Year) is fine; Songkran is for the Thai
  channel, not this one.
